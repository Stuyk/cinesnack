Recording

Record from any app.

Ctrl+Alt+K records the window you're in. Ctrl+K lets you pick a screen or window. You get a 5 second countdown, it records with your computer's audio, and it stops by itself at 5:00. Both shortcuts can be changed.

The recording bar

The bar stays in the corner where you can see it, and it's kept out of the video.

Stopping
  • Ctrl+Alt+K or Ctrl+K again, from any app
  • Click the red tray icon
  • Stop in the editor window
